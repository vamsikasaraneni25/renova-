THE BASICS

What Is Breast Health?

Breast health includes understanding the normal appearance and feel of your breasts, recognizing changes and knowing when to seek medical advice. Breasts naturally change throughout life due to age, hormones and other factors.

UNDERSTANDING

What Is Breast Cancer?

Breast cancer occurs when cells in breast tissue begin to grow uncontrollably. It can affect people of any sex, although it is much more common in women. Some cancers cause no noticeable symptoms in early stages.

Outcomes depend on many factors, including the type and stage of cancer.

RISK & AWARENESS

Factors That May Increase Risk

Having a risk factor does not mean someone will develop breast cancer, and cancer can occur without known risk factors.

Increasing Age

Risk generally increases with age.

Family History

Certain inherited variants and family patterns can increase risk.

Hormonal Factors

Some reproductive and hormonal exposures affect risk.

Lifestyle

Alcohol use and physical inactivity can contribute to risk.

Previous Exposure

Some prior chest radiation exposures increase risk.

EVERYDAY WELLNESS

Steps Toward Better Breast Health

No action can eliminate breast cancer risk, but some choices can support overall health.

Stay Physically Active

Find movement you enjoy and build it into your routine.

Eat a Balanced Diet

Choose varied foods including fruits, vegetables and whole grains.

Discuss Your Risk

Ask a healthcare professional about screening based on your circumstances.
